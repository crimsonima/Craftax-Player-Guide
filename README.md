# Craftax Player Handbook

Craftax is a survival game: gather resources, craft equipment, manage your needs, and descend through nine floors to the Necromancer. Keep these pages open while you play.

## Start here

| Step | Guide | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Your assignment](docs/study-details.md) | Getting the recorder, your participant ID, submitting recordings |
| 2 | [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md) | Launching and your first minutes of play |
| 3 | [Controls](docs/controls.md) | Every key |
| 4 | [Survival](docs/survival.md) | Food, water, energy, health and mana |
| 5 | [Recording and finishing](docs/recording.md) | Ending a session safely and finding your files |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To finish, close the **game window**, wait for **All saves completed safely.** in the console, then press Enter.
