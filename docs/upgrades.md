## Attributes

Strength, dexterity and intelligence start at 1 and go up to 5. Each level costs **1 XP**; you gain 1 XP the first time you reach each new floor.

| Attribute | Key | Each level gives | Max at level 1 → 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | - | +25% of the sword's base melee damage | Health 9 → 13 |
| Dexterity | ] | +1 arrow damage (+0.5 if the bow is enchanted); hunger, thirst and tiredness build up slower (half as fast at 5) | Food, drink, energy 9 → 17 |
| Intelligence | = | +1.5 spell damage; +5% sword enchantment damage; faster mana recovery | Mana 9 → 21 |

A higher maximum doesn't refill the resource. Damage at each level: [Combat](combat.md).

## Enchantments

| | Fire | Ice |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | 1 ruby + 9 mana | 1 sapphire + 9 mana |
| Where | Fire enchantment table (Vault) | Ice enchantment table (Sewers) |

- Face the table and press **K** (sword), **;** (bow) or **L** (armour).
- A sword or bow holds one element. Enchanting again replaces it, and repeating the same element still uses the gem and mana.
- **L** enchants one random armour piece, unenchanted pieces first.
- Upgrading a sword or armour piece keeps its enchantment.
- Use **ice** against Fire Realm enemies and **fire** against Ice Realm enemies; see [resistances](combat.md#enemy-resistances).

## Sword enchantment bonus

Extra fire or ice damage per hit, on top of the physical damage:

| Sword | Int 1 | Int 2 | Int 3 | Int 4 | Int 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Wood | 1 | 1.05 | 1.1 | 1.15 | 1.2 |
| Stone | 1.5 | 1.575 | 1.65 | 1.725 | 1.8 |
| Iron | 2.5 | 2.625 | 2.75 | 2.875 | 3 |
| Diamond | 4 | 4.2 | 4.4 | 4.6 | 4.8 |

Strength doesn't increase this bonus.
