# Getting started

[Home](../README.md) · [Controls](controls.md) · [Crafting](crafting.md) · [Help](troubleshooting.md)

## Launch

1. Unzip `CraftaxRecorder.zip` into a folder you own, such as Desktop or Documents (not Program Files).
2. Double-click `CraftaxRecorder.exe` in the `CraftaxRecorder` folder. If Windows shows **Windows protected your PC**, choose **More info → Run anyway**; the program isn't code-signed.
3. In the console, type your participant ID, press Enter, then type `y` to confirm. Later launches show the saved ID: press Enter to reuse it, after checking it's yours.
4. Wait for the game window. The first start, and your first key press, can each take up to a minute while the game prepares.
5. Click the game window once so it receives your keys.

## Basics

- The top of the window shows the world around you; the bottom shows health, food, drink, energy, mana, materials and equipment.
- **W/A/S/D** move and turn. **Space** acts on the tile you face: collect, mine, drink, open a chest, attack.
- The game advances only when you press a key; holding a key repeats it. **Q** waits one step.
- Sleeping (**Tab**) and resting (**E**) run by themselves until they end. They are not pauses.
- When health reaches zero, a new episode starts automatically and recording continues.

## Make your first equipment

1. Collect 3 wood: face a tree and press Space.
2. Press **T** to place a crafting table (2 wood).
3. Next to the table, press **1** for a wood pickaxe (1 wood).
4. Mine stone with Space.
5. With 1 wood + 1 stone each, press **2** for a stone pickaxe and **6** for a stone sword, next to the table.

"Next to" includes diagonals. All recipes: [Crafting](crafting.md).

## Stay alive

Drink by facing water and pressing Space. Eat by killing cows (or bats and snails) in melee, or by harvesting ripe plants. Sleep somewhere safe when energy is low. More in [Survival](survival.md).

## Next goals

Iron pickaxe and sword (need a table **and** a furnace nearby), armour, and torches for dark floors. Stand on a down ladder and press **.** to descend; below the Overworld, a ladder opens after 8 kills on that floor. See [Floors](world.md).
