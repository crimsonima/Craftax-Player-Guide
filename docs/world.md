|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Overworld | Zombies, skeletons |
| 2 | Dungeon | Orc soldiers, orc mages |
| 3 | Gnomish Mines | Gnome warriors, gnome archers |
| 4 | Sewers | Lizards, kobolds |
| 5 | Vault | Knights, archers |
| 6 | Troll Mines | Trolls, deep things |
| 7 | Fire Realm | Pigmen, fire elementals |
| 8 | Ice Realm | Frost trolls, ice elementals |
| 9 | Graveyard | The Necromancer and its waves |

## Moving between floors

Stand on a ladder and press **.** to go down or **,** to go up. Below the Overworld, each floor's down ladder opens after you defeat **8 enemies on that floor**.

Bring torches: the Gnomish Mines, Troll Mines, Ice Realm and Graveyard are dark. The Sewers have ice enchantment tables and the Vault fire enchantment tables.

> [!WARNING]
> There is no way back up from the Graveyard. Prepare before entering it.

## Resources by floor

Chance that each stone tile becomes the ore when the world is generated, so higher means more common:

| Floor | Coal | Iron | Diamond | Sapphire | Ruby |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Overworld | 3% | 2% | 0.1% | – | – |
| Gnomish Mines | 4% | 2% | 0.5% | 0.25% | 0.25% |
| Troll Mines | 4% | 3% | **1%** | 1% | 1% |
| Fire Realm | 5% | – | – | – | 2.5% |
| Ice Realm | – | – | 0.5% | 2% | – |

The Dungeon, Sewers, Vault and Graveyard have no ore, but chests can hold diamonds and other loot. The Overworld may have no diamonds at all.

Mining diamonds needs an **iron** pickaxe; sapphires and rubies need a **diamond** one. [Mining requirements](crafting.md#mining-requirements)
