## Needs

| Need | Restore it by | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Drink | Facing water and pressing Space (+1 each time) | Fill up before long trips |
| Food | Killing a cow, bat or snail in melee (+6), or harvesting a ripe plant (+4) | Kills with arrows or spells give no food |
| Energy | Sleeping (**Tab**) | Find a safe spot first |
| Health | Recovers over time while food, drink and energy aren't empty; **E** rests while injured | Resting doesn't feed you |
| Mana | Recovers over time, faster asleep | Spells cost 2, enchanting 9 |

All maximums start at 9 and grow with [attributes](upgrades.md#attributes).

Saplings come from interacting with grass. Plant them on grass with **P**; a ripe plant can be harvested again after it regrows.

## Sleep and rest

- **Tab** (when energy isn't full) sleeps until energy is full.
- **E** (when injured) rests until health is full or food or drink runs out.
- Both run by themselves and enemies can still attack. They are not pauses.

## Potions

Colours are reshuffled every episode, so test unknown potions only when you can afford to lose 3 health.

| Possible effect | Change |
|---|---:|
| Health | +8 or −3 |
| Mana | +8 or −3 |
| Energy | +8 or −3 |

## Before going down

Check food, drink, energy, health and arrows, and carry torches for dark floors. [Floors and resources](world.md)
