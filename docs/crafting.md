# Crafting and mining

[Home](../README.md) · [Controls](controls.md) · [Crafting](crafting.md) · [Help](troubleshooting.md)

Crafting needs a crafting table within the **8 tiles around you** (diagonals count); iron items also need a furnace within range. Stations aren't used up.

## Pickaxes and swords

| Item | Wood | Stone | Coal | Iron | Diamond | Furnace too |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| Wood pickaxe / sword | 1 | – | – | – | – | |
| Stone pickaxe / sword | 1 | 1 | – | – | – | |
| Iron pickaxe / sword | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | – | ✓ |
| Diamond pickaxe | 1 | – | – | – | 3 | |
| Diamond sword | 1 | – | – | – | 2 | |

A new tool replaces your current one. You can skip tiers, but you can't craft an equal or lower tier. [Keys](controls.md#craft-next-to-a-crafting-table) · [Damage](combat.md#melee-damage)

## Armour

| Press | Cost per piece | Furnace too |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Y**: iron piece | 3 iron + 3 coal | ✓ |
| **U**: diamond piece | 3 diamonds | |

Each press makes or upgrades **one** piece, in the order helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots. A full set takes 4 presses (12 iron + 12 coal, or 12 diamonds). Diamond pieces don't need iron ones first.

## Arrows and torches

| Press | Cost | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| **O** | 1 wood + 1 stone | 2 arrows |
| **[** | 1 wood + 1 coal | 4 torches |

## Placing

Table: 2 wood. Furnace: 1 stone. Stone block: 1 stone. Sapling: on grass. Torch: from your inventory. Each is placed on the free tile you face. Your first wood pickaxe therefore costs 3 wood, including the table.

## Mining requirements

| Resource | Minimum pickaxe |
|---|---|
| Wood (trees) | None |
| Stone, coal | Wood |
| Iron | Stone |
| Diamond | Iron |
| Sapphire, ruby | Diamond |

[Where to find ores](world.md#resources-by-floor)

## Not craftable

Bows, books and potions come from chests. Enchantment tables are found in the world; see [Enchantments](upgrades.md#enchantments).
